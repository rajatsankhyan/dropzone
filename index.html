How it works

Three steps. Then forget it.

Set it up once, auto-starts on every login, always ready in your menu bar.

1

Run on your laptop

One command starts the server and puts ⚡ in your Mac menu bar. A QR code appears in the terminal.

2

Scan once on your phone

Open the camera, scan the QR, and bookmark the URL. You'll never need the QR code again.

3

Transfer anything, instantly

Tap a file on your phone → saved to your laptop in seconds. Drag a file on your laptop → your phone gets a notification with a download link.

Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Built to be fast, private, and invisible until you need it.

📤

Phone → Laptop

Tap to pick any file — photo, video, PDF, anything. Lands in your uploads folder instantly.

📥

Laptop → Phone

Drag a file onto the laptop UI. Your phone gets a push notification with a one-tap download.

📋

Clipboard sync

Send text in both directions. Paste on laptop → appears on phone. Copy on phone → in laptop clipboard.

🔗

URL auto-open

Send a link from your phone — it lands in your laptop clipboard AND opens in your browser automatically.

🖼️

Image preview

Photos show as full-width thumbnails on mobile before you download. Know what you're getting.

🔐

PIN protected

A 4-digit PIN blocks anyone else on the WiFi. Enter once per device, remembered forever.

⚡

Local WiFi speed

No server in the middle. Files travel at full WiFi speed — 50–200 MB/s on a good network.

🍎

Menu bar & tray

Lives quietly in your Mac menu bar or Windows system tray. Shows connected devices live.

🚀

Auto-starts on boot

Run the installer once. DropZone starts automatically on every login — no terminal, ever.
